# --- shared Chat Completions-compatible helper: in-band error triage -------
providers/internal/openai_chat_completions_compatible.rs | serde_json::from_str::<serde_json::Value>(data).ok()?; | provider_response_from_compatible_sse_data deliberately runs pre-classification: compatible gateways send in-band error envelopes as data frames ("Treat the chunk as an error only when `error` is present AND carries a payload ... canonical OpenAI-compatible error event"), so it peeks for a populated `error` key (with no `choices`) to surface a CompletionError; unparseable or non-error chunks fall through to the classifier untouched.

# --- anthropic: content assembly, not frame triage -------------------------
providers/anthropic/streaming.rs | citation: serde_json::from_value(citation) | Field decode inside the content-delta Deserialize impl: converts the `citation` value of an already-tag-matched `citations_delta` into its typed form; failure is a serde::de error and unrecognized tags fall to Self::Unknown, so no frame is triaged here.
providers/anthropic/streaming.rs | serde_json::from_str(&server_tool_use.input_json) | Assembles the accumulated server_tool_use partial_json fragments into the raw-content value; malformed input surfaces as a CompletionError, no frame is triaged here.

# --- #[serde(untagged)] unions that are not frame triage --------------------
providers/openai/responses_api/streaming.rs | #[serde(untagged)] | Two sites, neither a triage fallback: `StreamingCompletionChunk` is the envelope union (response chunk vs item delta) decoded AFTER `classify_responses_frame` recognized the frame; `ContentPartChunkPart::Unknown` is untagged on the SERIALIZE side only — its Deserialize is the hand-written tag dispatch documented beside it, so a known tag with a defective payload still errors.
providers/openai/completion/streaming.rs | #[serde(untagged)] | `FinishReason::Other(String)` preserves an unrecognized finish-reason token verbatim (surfacing gateway-invented reasons instead of misreading them as a stop); a scalar vocabulary field, not frame policy.
